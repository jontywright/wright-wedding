Set among the vines of the Robertson valley, Duvon is a working wine estate with an open chapel that looks out over the mountains, and a pavilion built for long summer evenings. We could not imagine a more fitting place to begin our marriage.

Getting there

Open in Google Maps

From Cape Town

  1. Take the N1 east toward Worcester, roughly two hours.
  2. Continue on the R60 through Worcester toward Robertson.
  3. Follow signage for Duvon Wine Estate just outside Robertson.

From Robertson town

  1. Head out on the R60 in the direction of Ashton.
  2. Watch for the estate entrance on the vineyard side of the road.
  3. Park in the marked guest area and follow the path to the chapel.
