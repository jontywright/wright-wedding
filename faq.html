Questions

Frequently asked

Can we bring our children?

We have chosen to keep our day small and intimate, so our celebration is adults-only. We hope it gives you a relaxed evening to enjoy with us. Thank you for understanding.

Is there a bar?

Yes. There will be a cash bar open through the evening. Please bring cash or a card, as all drinks are for your own account.

What time should I arrive?

Please arrive by 15:00 and be seated by 15:20 so the bride can walk in at 15:30.

When do I need to RSVP?

Kindly RSVP by 31 August 2026 on the RSVP page. Each invitation is individual, so please reply for the name on your invite.

Can I bring a plus one?

Our guest list is individual. Only the names listed on the RSVP page are able to attend, so we are unable to accommodate additional plus ones.

Is the wedding indoors or outdoors?

The ceremony is in an open-air chapel overlooking the vines, and the reception is under a covered pavilion. Robertson in December can be very warm, so dress accordingly and stay hydrated. We will have water on the tables. Do bring a light layer for the evening once the sun goes down.

Where do I park?

There is ample parking at Duvon Wine Estate. Follow the signs and our parking attendants on the day.

Dietary needs or a song request?

You can note any dietary requirements and request up to three songs for the dance floor right inside your RSVP.
